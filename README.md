# Galen O'Shea-Stone, PhD

**Bioinformatics Scientist | Multiomics · Systems Biology**

I develop computational tools and analysis workflows to connect molecular measurements with biological questions. My work combines omics analysis, systems biology, statistical modeling, and scientific visualization in R and Python.

Embedded in the **Metabolomics and Lipidomics Unit at Rocky Mountain Laboratories, NIAID, NIH**, in Hamilton, Montana.

## Research and computational work

- **Metabolomics & lipidomics:** NMR- and mass spectrometry-based data analysis, preprocessing, quality control, and multivariate analysis.
- **Transcriptomics:** RNA-seq analysis, comparative gene expression, and pathway interpretation.
- **Multiomics integration:** Connecting metabolomic, lipidomic, and transcriptomic data to explore coordinated biological changes across molecular layers.
- **Scientific software:** Developing reusable analysis tools and visualizations that make computational methods accessible to biological researchers.
